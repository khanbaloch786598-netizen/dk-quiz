🎙 Narration in video (AI voice)

The downloaded video will also speak the questions aloud — intro, every question with its options, and the outro — just like popular quiz videos. The voice is generated inside your browser (free); ticking the box loads the voice engine right away (first use downloads the model once, ~30 MB, then it stays cached in this browser). Each voice starts exactly when its question appears and always finishes before the next question.

Narration works with fast export (Auto / Fast MP4 only). It adds extra time while the voices are generated — the status line shows the progress. If the voice model cannot load, the video is still created without narration.

Help

How to write your questions

Question text A) Option one B) Option two C) Option three D) Option four Answer: B (then the next question. Blank lines between questions are optional)

CSV / Excel also works, one question per line:

Question,Option A,Option B,Option C,Option D,B

Formats that are understood automatically

  • Numbered questions: 1. or Q1: or Question 2) (the number is removed).
  • Options: A) a. (a) A: A -, or all on one line: A) Red B) Blue C) Green D) Pink.
  • Answer: Answer: B, Ans B, Correct: (b), Answer: Islamabad (the option text), or put ✔ after the correct option.
  • Questions can run over several lines.
  • If a question cannot be understood, the page lists it under the text box so you can fix it.

Good to know

  • Maximum 200 questions per video, 2 to 4 options each.
  • Fast export builds a real MP4 file (H.264 + AAC) quicker than real time and plays in phone galleries, WhatsApp, YouTube and video editors. It needs a recent Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android. Keep the tab open until it finishes.
  • Compatibility mode records in real time. Use it only if fast export is not available.
  • Clock ticks, sound effects and background music are included in the video file. Pick a free built-in music track, or upload your own MP3 / WAV / M4A file in Audio & Voice.
  • The device voice reads questions aloud in Preview. For a voice inside the downloaded video, turn on 🎙 Narration in video in Audio & Voice — the voice is generated in your browser (English, free).
  • Available voices depend on your device. Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone each provide their own list.
  • For very long videos (100+ questions) use a computer and pick the Small file quality. Phones may run out of memory.
  • Download your video before closing the page.
